Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026 · this is a test version of LangRoll

Short version: you don't give us your name or email, we keep only what the app needs to track your progress, nothing is sold or shown to advertisers, and You → Delete my data erases it.

What we store

  • A guest account. The app creates it the first time you open it: a random ID, with no name, email or password.
  • Your settings: the language you're learning, your daily goal and your time zone (so your streak rolls over at your midnight).
  • What you do in lessons: which clips you've seen, your quiz answers (right first time or not), review grades, saves, duel results and speaking scores as numbers.
  • What we work out from that: your XP, streak and when each word comes back for review.
  • Feedback you send, with the app version and a device type like "iPhone Safari".

Speaking practice

When you tap the mic, your browser's own speech service turns what you say into text: Apple's on iPhone and in Safari, Google's in Chrome. LangRoll never receives the audio, only the score the app works out from the text.

Video clips

Some clips are short scenes from YouTube videos. Their pictures and the videos themselves come straight from YouTube, so YouTube (Google) receives those requests like any site it serves, under Google's privacy policy. Each scene links to the full video on YouTube.

Where it lives

  • Your guest account and progress: a database run by Supabase in the United States.
  • Our server, which checks and scores your progress: Fly.io, also in the US.
  • The app itself is served by GitHub Pages. Your phone also keeps a copy of your progress so the app works offline.

Connections are encrypted. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We don't use analytics yet. If we add usage analytics, this page will say so before it starts.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to You → Delete my data. Everything above is erased from our database straight away, and the app starts over with a new, unconnected guest account. You need to be online for this, so the app can confirm the deletion with our server.

Questions

Use You → Send feedback in the app. It reaches the team directly.
